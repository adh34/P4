Advocate for New Jersey Light Rail Expansion

Public transportation reduces traffic, helps the environment, and drives local economic growth. Use our resources and pre-written letters below to contact your local and state representatives and advocate for continued transit expansion across northern New Jersey.

Letter 01 · Hudson–Essex Regional Connection

Study Connecting Newark Light Rail & HBLR (West Side Ave / Ironbound)

Dear Representative, As a resident of [Your Municipality, NJ], I am writing to urge continued state and regional planning support for connecting New Jersey's existing light rail networks across Essex and Hudson counties. For nearly two centuries, the corridor connecting Newark Penn Station with downtown neighborhoods and neighboring communities has proven the unmatched value of dedicated transit rights-of-way. Yet Newark Light Rail and the Hudson-Bergen Light Rail (HBLR)—which already share compatible low-floor light rail vehicles—remain disconnected between West Side Avenue in Jersey City and Newark's Ironbound / Ferry Street corridor. Investing in feasibility studies, cross-river transit planning, and West Side Avenue westward expansion helps reduce highway congestion, lower regional emissions, and link workers directly to employment centers across both counties. Please prioritize multi-county light rail integration and capital funding for Newark Light Rail reliability and modernization. Sincerely, [Your Name] [Your Municipality, NJ]

Letter 02 · Essex–Passaic Corridor

Preserve Rail Rights-of-Way & Advance Newark–Paterson Transit Planning

Dear Representative, I am writing from [Your Municipality, NJ] to voice strong support for preserving existing rail rights-of-way and evaluating northward light rail or high-frequency transit extensions linking Newark, Bloomfield, Belleville, and Paterson. The success of the 2002 Grove Street extension and 2006 Broad Street extension demonstrated how modest, well-targeted light rail investments attract daily ridership, connect university campuses, and spur neighborhood revitalization. Extending rapid, accessible transit connections northward toward Passaic County would give thousands of commuters a reliable car-free alternative along one of New Jersey's densest corridors. I respectfully ask you to support NJ Transit capital planning, corridor preservation, and state/federal grant applications aimed at expanding light rail service in northern New Jersey. Sincerely, [Your Name] [Your Municipality, NJ]

Letter 03 · Airport & Neighborhood Access

Improve Transit Access to Newark Airport (EWR), Ironbound & Elizabeth

Dear Representative, As a resident of [Your Municipality, NJ], I urge your support for expanded public transit access serving Newark's Ironbound, South Ward, Elizabeth, and Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR). While the Broad Street Extension completed the first phase of the Newark-Elizabeth Rail Link in 2006, neighborhoods south of Newark Penn Station still lack direct local light rail service. Alongside ongoing Newark Airport rail station access improvements, strengthening local rapid-transit links between downtown Newark, Ferry Street, and EWR creates fair job access for airport workers and reduces traffic along Routes 1 & 9 and I-78. Thank you for championing sustained operating and capital funding for New Jersey public transportation. Sincerely, [Your Name] [Your Municipality, NJ]
