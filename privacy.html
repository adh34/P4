Data & Privacy

Privacy Statement

1. No Tracking or Cookies

The NLR History website does not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or commercial advertising beacons. Browsing this site is entirely anonymous.

2. Contact Form & Communications

Our submission form operates statically on client-side JavaScript by generating a direct email draft using your system's default email client. Information entered into form fields (such as your name, email, and historical inquiry) is never stored in a central database or sold to third parties.

3. External Links

This website contains links to external educational, historical, and government websites (including NJ Transit, the New Jersey Legislature, and Wikimedia Commons). These external sites have separate and independent privacy policies. We encourage visitors to review the privacy statements of any third-party websites they visit.

4. Questions

For any questions regarding this privacy statement or the NLR History student project, contact Aidan Heaney at adh34@njit.edu.
