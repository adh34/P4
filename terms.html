Terms of Service

1. Educational Archive Purpose

The Newark Light Rail History (NLR History) website is an independent educational and historical research project developed for academic presentation at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). It is created strictly for non-commercial educational purposes.

2. Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

This website is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by New Jersey Transit (NJ Transit), the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey, Public Service Enterprise Group (PSEG), or any government transportation agency. All trademarks, service marks, and trade names referenced on this site belong to their respective owners.

3. Informational Accuracy & Transit Schedules

Historical narratives, dates, maps, and technical dossiers are compiled for research purposes. This website does not provide official live transit schedules or real-time service advisories. Commuters seeking current schedules or fares must visit official agency websites such as njtransit.com.

4. Archival Media & Copyright

Photographs, illustrations, and maps displayed in the archive are utilized under public domain designations, Creative Commons licenses (CC BY-SA), or with explicit permissions as attributed in photo captions. If you hold rights to any historical media displayed and request updated attribution or removal, please contact the maintainer via the Contact page.
