Operations & Network Reference

About the Newark Light Rail System

The Newark Light Rail operates across two main segments connecting major transit hubs, commercial districts, and local university campuses. This guide outlines the current system footprint, station directory, rolling stock, and daily operational profile.

6.2 mi Total Track Network (10.0 km)
17 Total Light Rail Stations
2 Branches from Newark Penn
21 LRVs Kinki Sharyo Low-Floor Fleet

Schematic line overview. For official real-time schedules, fares, and service advisories, consult NJ Transit directly.

Branch 1: Grove Street Line (City Subway Corridor)

Running approximately 5.3 miles northwest from downtown Newark Penn Station through Branch Brook Park and Belleville into Bloomfield, this segment follows the historic #7 City Subway alignment—underground beneath Raymond Boulevard from Newark Penn to Washington Street, then emerging into the landscaped former Morris Canal cut and continuing onto surface right-of-way to Grove Street.

Branch 2: Broad Street Line

Covering roughly 1.0 mile through downtown Newark, the Broad Street Extension connects Newark Penn Station to Newark Broad Street Station along street-level trackage, featuring a split one-way couplet serving Atlantic Street & Riverfront Stadium outbound and Harriet Tubman Square (formerly Washington Park) inbound.

Fleet, Fares & Accessibility

All 17 modern stations feature low-floor platforms providing step-free ADA boarding onto the 21 articulated Kinki Sharyo vehicles. Ticket vending machines and validators operate on a proof-of-payment system across platforms prior to boarding.
